Colophon

The ridges are real.

The topographic contours drifting through the hero and the contact section are not procedural. They trace actual California terrain, extracted once from a public elevation raster and shipped as static geometry — no map library, no tiles, no runtime fetches of the raw data.

Elevation data
NASA SRTM GL3  (3‑arcsecond, ~90 m) · courtesy of NASA / JPL and USGS, distributed via OpenTopography.
Snapshots
Hero: Central California (−122.5° to −115.0°, 33° to 40°). Contact: Mojave & Death Valley (−120° to −114.5°, 32.6° to 37.2°).
Pipeline
Python · PIL downsample · 3×3 box blur · matplotlib marching-squares at 130 m / 650 m intervals · Ramer–Douglas–Peucker simplification · SVG + JSON output.
Runtime
Vite + vanilla JS. A canvas spring field reads the pre-generated polylines and deforms them under the cursor.

Simple Icons, served tinted; AWS & CSS3 inlined as hand-crafted monochrome marks.
